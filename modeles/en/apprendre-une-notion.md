Explain this concept to me: [to be completed: the concept, and what I already know about it].

- Start with an explanation in three sentences, without jargon, with an image or a comparison.
- Then go deeper in three steps, each with a concrete example.
- Finish with a short exercise and its answer.

At the end, check that I have understood by asking me a question.

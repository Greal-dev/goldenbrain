    Read every document of the tender file, skipping none. List: the subject and scope of the contract, the
    award criteria and their weight, the requirements (with the document and page they come from), the
    deliverables requested, key dates, traps and contradictions between documents, questions to ask the
    buyer. Mark what is certain, what is inferred and what is missing.
  entrees: All documents of the folder processed (rules, specifications, appendices, price schedule).
  livrable: tender-analysis.md
- titre: Value proposition
  consigne: |
    From the analysis, state what the buyer really wants (their problem, their risks, what will make them
    choose) and how our offer answers it better than another: three to five win themes, each tied to an
    award criterion and to a proof. Point out the weaknesses to cover.
  entrees: Deliverable of the tender analysis.
  livrable: value-proposition.md
  validation: true
- titre: Solution
  consigne: |
    Describe the proposed solution: organisation, approach, resources, schedule, commitments, with for each
    requirement of the analysis the answer given (requirement → answer matrix). Keep to the approved value
    proposition. Promise nothing that cannot be kept; list the assumptions.
  entrees: Tender analysis and value proposition.
  livrable: solution.md
- titre: Writing
  consigne: |
    Write the technical proposal in the outline imposed by the tender (otherwise in the order of the
    criteria), using the solution and the win themes. Clear sentences, figures as proof, no copy-paste of
    the specifications.
  entrees: Solution, value proposition, template from the tender if any.
  livrable: technical-proposal.md
- titre: Proofreading and correction
  consigne: |
    Read the proposal as the evaluator would: compliance with each requirement and with the imposed outline,

    is certain and list separately what needs a decision. End with the list of documents to submit.
  entrees: Technical proposal, tender analysis.
  livrable: technical-proposal-reviewed.md and documents-to-submit.md
